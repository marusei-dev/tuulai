## How to use

The nominative is the plain dictionary form of a noun. It takes **no suffix** and
answers the questions **хэн?** (who?) and **юу?** (what?). The subject of a sentence
is in the nominative case.

| Асуулт | Жишээ      |
| ------ | ---------- |
| Хэн?   | Дорж, багш |
| Юу?    | ном, нохой |

Mongolian has **8 cases** in total. Every other case adds a suffix to this plain form.
