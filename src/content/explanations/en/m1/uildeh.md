## How to use

The instrumental shows **how** or **with what** something is done. It answers
**юугаар?** (with what? by what?).

| Хэрэглээ  | Жишээ                                         |
| --------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| Transport | автобусаар – by bus                           |
| Tool      | үзгээр – with a pen                           |
| Language  | монголоор, англиар – in Mongolian, in English |

The four forms follow the same vowel harmony as the ablative (-аар, -ээр, -оор,
-өөр). After a long vowel a **г** is added: дүү → дүү**гээр**.
